About Sift Sweets

Sift Sweets is a small homemade bakery in Texas. What started in 2022 as baking for friends and family turned into custom orders, market tables and a whole lot of flour on the kitchen floor.

This site is where our recipes live. Some are customer favorites straight from our order list, some are weekend projects and some are the desserts we make when we want to show off a little. Every one has been tested in a regular home kitchen so you can bake it in yours.

What you will find here

Cookies, cupcakes, celebration cakes and chocolate everything, written with weights and cups, plenty of notes and honest tips for when things go sideways.
